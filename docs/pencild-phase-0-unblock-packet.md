## Part C — Draft: Support page (for getpencild.com/support)

> **Pencil'd Support**
>
> Pencil'd turns a photo or screenshot of an event flyer, invitation, or ticket into a calendar event. Pencil'd reads the title, date, time, and venue from the image; you review and edit everything before anything is saved; then the event goes to Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders with the alerts you choose.
>
> Everything happens on your device. Pencil'd has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking. Your photos and the text in them never leave your device.
>
> **How it works**
> 1. Take a photo of a paper flyer, or pick a screenshot or photo from your library.
> 2. Pencil'd fills in the event title, date, time, and venue it finds.
> 3. Review and edit — nothing is saved until you approve it.
> 4. Save to Calendar and Reminders, with alerts.
>
> **Plans**
> - **Free:** scan one screenshot, photo, or paper flyer at a time; review and edit; save with alerts; keep a history/archive.
> - **Plus:** adds automatic detection of new event screenshots on the Home screen and "Likely events" highlighting.
> - **Premium:** adds PDF and Files import (every page), possible-duplicate detection across your archive, deadline reminders, and tailored alert plans.
> - Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled anytime in iOS Settings under your Apple Account's Subscriptions. New subscribers get a 1-week free trial on Plus and Premium.
>

## Part D — Draft: Privacy page (for getpencild.com/privacy)

> **Pencil'd Privacy Policy**
>
> Pencil'd does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and Pencil'd runs no servers. Photos you scan and the information read from them are processed on your device and never leave it.
>
> **What Pencil'd asks permission for, and why**
> - **Photo Library:** so you can pick a screenshot or photo of an event to scan.
> - **Camera:** only when you choose "Scan a paper flyer."
> - **Calendars and Reminders:** so events you review and approve can be saved, with the alerts you set.
> - **Notifications:** so those alerts can reach you.
>
> Events, reminders, and your scan history live on your device (and in your own Calendar and Reminders). Deleting the app removes Pencil'd's own stored data.
